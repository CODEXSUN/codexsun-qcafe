# Operate a deployment

Read `assist/architecture/architecture.md` and the deployment decision record.

Resolve the exact application, release, organization, provider, and instance before a mutation.

Inspect current desired and observed state. Do not repeat a completed transition.

Require approval for destructive cleanup, public release, or costly scale changes.

Verify health, logs, and recorded state after a transition.
